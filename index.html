Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers about our bulk fuel supply

What types of fuel do you supply?

We supply bulk diesel, petrol and illuminating paraffin, together with premium lubricants for commercial and industrial use.

Do you offer delivery services?

Yes. We deliver primarily across the Eastern Cape, with other locations considered according to order requirements and availability.

What is the minimum order size?

Bulk fuel orders start from 4,000 litres. Contact our team with your product, quantity and delivery location for a quotation.

How quickly can you deliver?

Approved orders are scheduled for delivery within 48 hours, subject to product availability and delivery requirements.

How do you ensure the quality of your fuel?

We source from established suppliers and offer on-site product testing to support quality assurance.

How do I request a quotation?

Call us on +27 11 243 5454 or email info@liquital.co.za with the product, volume and delivery location you require.
