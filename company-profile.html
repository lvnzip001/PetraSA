About FuelAxis

A dependable fuel partner, not simply a supplier.

Clear procurement. Coordinated delivery. Products matched to real operational demand.

FuelAxis is a licensed wholesaler and distributor of bulk diesel, petrol and illuminating paraffin, together with premium lubricants for commercial and industrial customers. Operated under Ultralucent and led by Chief Executive Officer Mizamo Mjekula, we focus on dependable supply and direct communication.

We deliver primarily across the Eastern Cape, with other locations considered according to order needs and availability. Our approach is straightforward: understand the operation, confirm the requirement and coordinate a reliable supply solution.
