FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to plan your order.

Clear answers about product availability, order sizes, delivery and quality support.

What products does FuelAxis supply?

We supply bulk diesel, petrol and illuminating paraffin, together with premium lubricants for commercial and industrial use.

What is the minimum bulk order?

Bulk fuel orders start from 4,000 litres. Contact our team with your product, quantity and delivery location for a quotation.

Where does FuelAxis deliver?

We deliver primarily across the Eastern Cape. Other locations are considered according to order requirements and availability.

How quickly can an approved order be delivered?

Approved orders are planned around a 48-hour target, subject to product availability, account approval and delivery requirements.

Is product testing available?

Yes. We offer on-site product testing to support quality assurance at your premises.
